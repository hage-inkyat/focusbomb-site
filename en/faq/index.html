Frequently asked questions

Basics

What is Focus Bomb?

A focus timer that broadly restricts supported apps and websites during a session. The less you touch your phone, the higher your score.

Is it free?

Yes. The Free plan has no limit on how many times you seal, and no ads. Emergency release is always free and works offline.

Restrictions

Does this really block every app?

No. Focus Bomb restricts a broad range of supported apps and websites, but due to iOS system constraints, phone calls and some system functions are outside what any app can restrict.

Can I still make phone calls?

Yes — regular phone calls remain available during a focus session. See the Safety page for the exact iOS-level constraints.

Safety

How do I release early in an emergency?

Hold the emergency release button — always visible during a session and in the Warning Room — for 7 seconds. It's free and works offline.

Can I still finish a session if your servers are down?

Yes. Release logic is independent of our servers, ads, and billing, and continues/releases locally.

Puzzles

Is the puzzle the same every time?

No. It's freshly generated from a deterministic seed each time, and verified to always have exactly one correct wire.

Can I solve it if I can't distinguish colors?

Yes. Pattern, symbol, and port number are used alongside color, so nothing depends on color alone.

Scoring

How much time do I lose for a wrong answer?

Your remaining time is multiplied by 1.5×. The extension never exceeds the absolute maximum end time shown before you start.

How is Focus Score calculated?

It's based on session duration and outcome (no-touch completion, warning-room-only, hint viewed, early defusal, or failure). Ads and purchases never add points.

Ads

Are ads shown during a session?

No. We never show ads during a session, in the Warning Room, on the puzzle screen, or around emergency release.

Subscription

What's the difference between Free and Pro?

Free has no limit on how many times you seal, and no ads. Emergency release is always free and works offline. What Pro adds is four things: unlimited schedules, recipes you write yourself, history beyond 30 days, and detailed analytics.

What happens if I cancel?

Your history is never deleted. Pro-only features revert to Free limits, but your settings remain.

Teams

If a teammate drops out mid-session, does my time extend too?

No. Every device releases independently — nobody else's actions can change your own lock time.

Privacy

Is my focus history or restricted app list sent to your servers?

No. Your focus history stays primarily on-device, and restricted app names are never sent to our servers.

Troubleshooting

It looks like I'm not being released — what do I do?

First try the 7-second emergency release hold. If that doesn't resolve it, contact us via Support.

Creators

Can I join as a Creator?

Creator features are in development. See the Creators page for details.
