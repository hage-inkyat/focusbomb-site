Ad differences

The Free plan shows ads, e.g. after a results screen. Pro removes forced ads entirely. Neither plan ever shows ads during a session, in the Warning Room, on the puzzle screen, or around emergency release.

Free trial

The annual plan's free trial is shown and applied only when it's actually active in App Store Connect. The monthly plan has no trial.

Auto-renewal & cancellation

Purchases and subscription management happen through the App Store. Subscriptions renew automatically.

Cancel anytime from your App Store subscription settings in the Settings app. Pro features remain available through the end of the current billing period.

Restoring purchases

After a device change, use "Restore Purchases" in Focus Bomb's Settings to restore Pro.

Regional pricing

The App Store shows pricing localized to your region.

iOS only (Android in the future)

We currently offer iOS only. If an Android version ships in the future, purchases will be managed separately per store — an iOS purchase does not automatically carry over to Android.
