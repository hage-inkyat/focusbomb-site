Notifications aren't a walkthrough — they're temptation.

Every 15 minutes, one more hint unlocks. Tapping the notification brings you closer to a manual release, but opening the bomb screen at all lowers your Focus Score. Ignore it, and you're auto-released at the planned time.

Your focus history stays on your iPhone as much as possible.

Your focus history and restricted-app data stay on the device. The only thing v1.0 sends to a server is a configuration fetch at launch — never what you were focusing on.

  • No email required
  • We don't access your contacts
  • We don't collect precise location
  • Restricted app names are never sent to our servers
  • We don't collect what you were working on

There are no ads in v1.0. No advertising SDK is bundled in the app, so there is no advertising identifier and nothing to track.
