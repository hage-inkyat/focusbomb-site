Start the session, then put your phone down.

1. The Focus Bomb principle

Nothing happens if you don't touch it. Ignoring notifications scores higher, by design.

2. Starting a session

Pick a duration starting at 15 minutes, and review both the planned end time and the absolute maximum end time before you start.

3. What gets restricted

A broad range of supported apps and websites. Some functions, like phone calls, remain available under iOS's own constraints.

4. Notifications

A silent temptation notification arrives every 15 minutes. Ignoring it doesn't break anything.

5. The Warning Room

Tapping a notification opens the Warning Room. Choosing to return to focus keeps you safe, though you lose the no-touch bonus.

6. The puzzle

Opening the bomb screen shows a puzzle with exactly one correct wire, never dependent on color alone.

7. Penalty

Cutting the wrong wire multiplies your remaining time by 1.5x. The extension never exceeds the absolute maximum end time.

8. Auto-release

The shield clears automatically once the planned time arrives.

9. Focus Score

Calculated from duration and outcome (no-touch, warning-room-only, hint-viewed, early defusal, or failure).

10. Emergency release

Always available, free, and offline — hold for 7 seconds.

11. If we're offline

Your session continues and releases locally even if our servers, ads, or billing are down.
